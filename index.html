A place for every commitment.

Personal Organizer is a personal productivity integration being developed to manage tasks, routines, and dated commitments through Codex skills and the services you already use.

How it works

Linear
Projects, milestones, and tasks, including context, priorities, and progress.
Habitica
Recurring routines, practice instructions, habits, and recorded completions.
Google Calendar
Appointments, birthdays, payment deadlines, and scheduled events. Google Calendar remains the source of truth for dated commitments.

Google Calendar access

The integration is intended for its owner's personal use. After the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, local tools can read, create, update, and delete calendar events in response to their requests. Event details may include titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations, reminders, and attendees.

This website provides information only. It has no sign-in form and does not connect to your calendars. Google authorization takes place separately through the local integration.

Read the privacy policy for details about data access, processing, and revoking permissions.
